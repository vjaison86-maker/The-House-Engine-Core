# Purpose

The Pattern Library™ defines how recurring human experiences, behaviours, beliefs, and responses are identified, understood, and connected within The House Engine™.

Patterns help a person observe the invisible structures influencing their thoughts, choices, and experiences.

The purpose of identifying patterns is not to judge behaviour.

The purpose is to create awareness.

---

# Core Principle

## Awareness Before Change

A pattern cannot be consciously changed until it can first be recognised.

The House Engine™ does not treat patterns as problems to eliminate.

Patterns are signals that help a person understand themselves.

---

# What Is A Pattern?

A pattern is a recurring way of:

- thinking
- feeling
- behaving
- interpreting experiences
- responding to situations

A pattern may operate consciously or unconsciously.

---

# Pattern Philosophy

Patterns are:

- observations, not labels
- invitations for exploration, not conclusions
- possibilities, not fixed identities

A person is not their pattern.

A pattern is something a person can observe.

---

# Pattern Structure

Every pattern should contain:

---

## Pattern Name

A clear and understandable name.

Example:

Autopilot Living™

---

## Purpose

Why this pattern exists in The House Engine™.

Example:

Help a person recognise unconscious routines and choices.

---

## Description

What the pattern represents.

---

## Experience

How this pattern may appear in someone's life.

Examples:

- repeated behaviours
- emotional responses
- decision habits
- internal conflicts

---

## Awareness Shift

The movement created by understanding the pattern.

Example:

FROM:

"I am stuck and do not know why."

TO:

"I can observe the patterns influencing my choices."

---

## Reflection Questions

Questions that help explore the pattern.

Questions should:

- create curiosity
- avoid judgement
- encourage self-observation

---

## Connected Practices

Practices that help a person explore or respond to the pattern.

---

## Connected Journeys

Journeys where this pattern may appear.

---

## Mirror Guidance

How The Mirror™ should discuss this pattern.

The Mirror™ should:

- introduce gently
- invite exploration
- allow personal interpretation

The Mirror™ should not:

- diagnose
- assign the pattern
- define the person

# Pattern Validation

Before adding a new pattern, evaluate:

## Relevance

Does this represent a meaningful human experience?

## Clarity

Can people understand the concept?

## Usefulness

Does it create reflection or insight?

## Alignment

Does it match The House Engine™ principles?
