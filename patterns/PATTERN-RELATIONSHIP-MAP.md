# Purpose

The Pattern Relationship Map™ defines how patterns within The House Engine™ connect, influence, and relate to each other.

The purpose is not to create a fixed psychological model.

The purpose is to help The Mirror™ understand that human experiences often involve multiple connected patterns.

---

# Core Principle

## Patterns Exist In Relationship

A person rarely experiences a single pattern in isolation.

Patterns may:

- influence each other
- reinforce each other
- appear together
- create opportunities for awareness

Understanding relationships creates deeper reflection.

---

# Pattern Relationship Philosophy

Patterns are:

- connected experiences
- areas of exploration
- signals for awareness

Patterns are not:

- diagnoses
- categories of identity
- permanent characteristics

A person can observe and transform their relationship with patterns.

# Pattern Relationships

## Autopilot Living™ → Identity Attachment™

### Relationship

Automatic living may be influenced by unconscious identities.

A person may continue following a path because it has become part of how they define themselves.

### Reflection Direction

Explore:

"What identities influence my choices without my awareness?"

---

## Identity Attachment™ → External Validation™

### Relationship

A person may seek external recognition to maintain an identity they feel attached to.

Example:

"I need to continue achieving because achievement defines who I am."

### Reflection Direction

Explore:

"Am I choosing this identity, or protecting it?"

---

## External Validation™ → Avoidance Pattern™

### Relationship

Fear of judgement or rejection may create avoidance of situations where approval feels uncertain.

Example:

"A person avoids trying something new because failure may affect how others see them."

### Reflection Direction

Explore:

"What am I avoiding because I fear how it may be perceived?"

---

## Avoidance Pattern™ → Autopilot Living™

### Relationship

Avoiding difficult reflection or decisions may keep existing patterns unchanged.

Example:

"A person continues familiar routines because questioning them feels uncomfortable."

### Reflection Direction

Explore:

"What patterns continue because they have never been examined?"
