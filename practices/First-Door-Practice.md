# Purpose

The First Door Practice™ is the first intentional act of self-exploration inside The House Engine™.

Its purpose is not to solve a person's life.

Its purpose is to create the first moment of awareness.

Before someone can redesign their inner architecture, they must first become willing to observe it.

# Core Principle

## The First Step Is Awareness

Transformation begins when a person becomes willing to look within.

---

# The Practice

## Opening The First Door

Take a quiet moment.

Remove the expectation to find immediate answers.

Simply observe.

Ask yourself:

"What inside me have I never taken time to understand?"

---

# Reflection Process

## Step 1 — Pause

Create a moment without distraction.

Notice:

- your thoughts
- your emotions
- your current state
- the questions appearing inside you

---

## Step 2 — Observe

Do not judge what you discover.

Simply notice:

- recurring thoughts
- repeated behaviours
- emotional reactions
- areas of confusion
- things you avoid exploring

---

## Step 3 — Write

Complete these sentences:

"I have been carrying..."

"I often find myself..."

"A part of me wants to understand..."

"I have avoided looking at..."

---

## Step 4 — Recognize

Ask:

"What pattern might this reveal about me?"

---
