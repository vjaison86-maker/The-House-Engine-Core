## Status

Version: 0.1

Stage: Foundation

---

# Purpose

The Product Architecture Framework™ defines how The House Engine™ knowledge can be transformed into meaningful products and experiences.

Products are not created separately from the knowledge system.

They emerge from the transformation pathways inside The House Engine™.

---

# Core Principle

## Knowledge Before Product

A product should emerge from a validated understanding of:

- human experiences
- patterns
- questions
- practices
- transformation journeys

The goal is not to create more content.

The goal is to create useful transformation experiences.

# Product Categories

## 1. Reflection Products

Purpose:

Help people explore themselves.

Examples:

- journals
- reflection workbooks
- question decks
- guided prompts

Connected Layers:

Questions + Practices

---

## 2. Learning Products

Purpose:

Help people understand concepts.

Examples:

- books
- courses
- workshops
- learning programs

Connected Layers:

Rooms + Patterns + Journeys

---

## 3. Practice Products

Purpose:

Help people convert awareness into action.

Examples:

- exercises
- challenges
- routines
- guided experiences

Connected Layers:

Practices

---

## 4. AI Experiences

Purpose:

Provide interactive guidance through The Mirror™.

Examples:

- AI reflection companion
- guided self-discovery assistant
- personal insight system

Connected Layers:

Mirror + Knowledge Graph

# Product Design Principles

## 1. Transformation Over Information

Products should help people move from:

Awareness

↓

Understanding

↓

Action

---

## 2. Experience Over Content

A product should create interaction, not just provide information.

---

## 3. Simplicity Before Complexity

Start with the smallest useful experience.

Expand only after learning from users.

---

# Product Validation

Before creating a product, evaluate:

## Problem

Does this solve a real human need?

## Alignment

Does it connect to The House Engine™ principles?

## Transformation

Does it create meaningful movement?

## Experience

Can a person use it easily?

---

# Product Connection Model

Every product should connect to:

Room:

Where does the experience begin?

Pattern:

What challenge does it address?

Questions:

What reflection does it create?

Practice:

What action does it encourage?

Journey:

Where does it guide the person?

Mirror:

How does AI support it?
