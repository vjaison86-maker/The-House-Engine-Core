## Status

Version: 0.1

Stage: Foundation

# Purpose

The Research Framework™ defines how knowledge is explored, evaluated, and integrated into The House Engine™.

The purpose is to ensure that concepts, patterns, questions, practices, and products are developed with thoughtful investigation and continuous learning.

Research supports the system.

Research does not replace human experience.

---

# Core Principle

## Understanding Before Integration

A concept should not become part of The House Engine™ only because it is interesting.

It should be evaluated through:

- relevance
- usefulness
- alignment
- evidence
- human experience

# Research Evaluation Model

Before integrating a concept, evaluate:

## 1. Relevance

Does this help understand human experience?

---

## 2. Alignment

Does this support The House Engine™ principles?

---

## 3. Practical Value

Can this knowledge create useful reflection or action?

---

## 4. Evidence Quality

What level of support exists for this concept?

---

## 5. Human Impact

Does this help people develop awareness and conscious choice?

# Research Categories

## Pattern Research

Purpose:

Understand recurring human behaviours and experiences.

Examples:

- avoidance patterns
- identity attachment
- unconscious habits

---

## Question Research

Purpose:

Develop questions that create meaningful reflection.

Questions should encourage:

- awareness
- curiosity
- exploration

---

## Practice Research

Purpose:

Explore exercises that help convert awareness into action.

Examples:

- reflection exercises
- journaling
- behavioural experiments

---

## Journey Research

Purpose:

Understand transformation pathways.

Examples:

- awareness stages
- learning progression
- personal growth processes

---

# Research Standards

The House Engine™ should avoid:

- unsupported claims
- forced conclusions
- universal assumptions about people

The system should encourage:

- curiosity
- exploration
- personal interpretation
