# The House Engine™ Architecture

Version: 1.0 (Blueprint)

---

# Vision

The House Engine™ is a living knowledge architecture for human self-understanding.

It transforms wisdom from The House Within™ into an interconnected intelligence system that can support reflection, coaching, AI conversations, learning journeys, and future digital experiences.

The goal is not simply to store knowledge.

The goal is to organize human transformation.

---

# Core Philosophy

The House Engine™ is built around one belief:

People do not need more information.

People need deeper understanding.

Every component inside this system exists to help a person move from unconscious living toward intentional living.
