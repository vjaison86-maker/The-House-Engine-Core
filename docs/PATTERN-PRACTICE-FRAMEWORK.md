## Status

Version: 0.1

Stage: Foundation

# Purpose

The Pattern Practice Connection Framework™ defines how identified patterns within The House Engine™ connect to meaningful practices.

The purpose is to create a bridge between awareness and intentional action.

A pattern creates awareness.

A practice creates an opportunity to respond differently.

---

# Core Principle

## Awareness Creates Possibility

The House Engine™ does not aim to remove patterns through force.

It aims to help a person:

- notice patterns
- understand their influence
- explore new responses
- make conscious choices

---

# Practice Philosophy

Practices are:

- intentional exercises
- reflection experiences
- awareness-building activities
- opportunities for experimentation

Practices are not:

- instructions for how someone must live
- solutions imposed on a person
- replacements for personal judgement
- guarantees of transformation

Patterns often operate automatically.

Awareness creates a pause.

Practices create a space where a person can explore a different response.

# Practice Design Principles

## 1. Awareness Before Action

A practice should begin with understanding.

The person should know:

- what they are exploring
- why it matters
- what they want to observe

---

## 2. Small Steps Over Forced Change

Practices should encourage manageable exploration.

The goal is not:

"Completely change yourself."

The goal is:

"Understand yourself through intentional action."

---

## 3. Reflection Creates Learning

A practice is incomplete without reflection.

The important question is not only:

"What did I do?"

but:

"What did I learn about myself?"

---

## 4. Personal Agency

Practices should support personal choice.

The person remains the authority over:

- their interpretation
- their decisions
- their timing

---

# Practice Structure

Each practice within The House Engine™ should contain:

---

## Practice Identity

Name:

Example:

First Door Practice™

Purpose:

What awareness or exploration does this practice support?

---

## Connected Pattern

The pattern this practice relates to.

Example:

Pattern:

Autopilot Living™

---

## Practice Intention

What the person is invited to explore.

Example:

"Notice moments where actions happen automatically."

---

## Practice Process

The activity or reflection experience.

Example:

Pause during daily routines and ask:

"Am I choosing this consciously?"

---

## Reflection

Questions that help extract learning.

Example:

"What did I notice that I had previously ignored?"

---

## Integration

How the learning connects with future choices.

Purpose:

## Avoidance Pattern™

# Mirror Guidance Rules

The Mirror™ should:

- explain the purpose
- encourage reflection
- respect personal choice

The Mirror™ should not:

- prescribe behaviour
- force completion
- judge outcomes
- assume readiness
