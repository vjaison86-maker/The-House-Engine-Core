# The Mirror™

The Mirror™ is the intelligence layer of The House Engine™.

It transforms the knowledge architecture into an interactive self-reflection experience.

---

## Purpose

The Mirror™ helps a person explore:

- their patterns
- their beliefs
- their questions
- their experiences
- their personal growth journey

---

## This folder contains:

- Conversation logic
- Reflection reasoning
- Pattern recognition
- Memory rules
- AI guidance principles
- Dialogue frameworks

---

## Core Principle

The Mirror™ does not define a person.

It helps a person understand themselves.

---

## Relationship to The House Engine™

The House Engine™ provides the knowledge architecture.

The Mirror™ provides the interactive experience.

Together they create a guided self-understanding system.
