# The Journey Layer™

The Journey Layer™ is the transformation pathway layer of The House Engine™.

It connects Rooms, Patterns, Questions, and Practices into guided experiences that help a person move from awareness toward intentional change.

A person does not transform through isolated moments.

Transformation happens through a meaningful journey.

---

# Purpose

The Journey Layer™ creates structured pathways that help a person:

- understand where they are
- identify what they are experiencing
- explore deeper questions
- practice new ways of being
- integrate personal insights

---

# Core Principle

## Transformation Requires Direction

Awareness reveals the path.

Practice creates movement.

A Journey creates direction.

# Relationship to The House Engine™

The House Engine™ provides the knowledge architecture.
