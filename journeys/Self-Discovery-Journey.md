# Purpose

The Self-Discovery Journey™ is the first transformation pathway inside The House Engine™.

Its purpose is to guide a person from unconscious living toward deeper awareness, self-understanding, and intentional choice.

This journey does not tell a person who they are.

It helps them discover what already exists within them.

# Journey Overview

Every transformation begins with a question:

"Who am I beneath the roles, expectations, and patterns that shape my life?"

The Self-Discovery Journey™ creates a structured path for exploring that question.

---

# Starting Point

A person entering this journey may experience:

- feeling disconnected from themselves
- uncertainty about direction
- repeating patterns they do not understand
- feeling controlled by expectations
- searching for meaning

They may not know exactly what needs to change.

They only know they want deeper understanding.

### Questions
