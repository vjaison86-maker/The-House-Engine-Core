# Entrance Room™

## Status

Version: 0.1  
Stage: Foundation

---

# Purpose

The Entrance Room™ is the first doorway into The House Engine™.

Its purpose is to help a person pause, become aware of their inner world, and begin the journey of understanding themselves.

Before transformation can happen, awareness must begin.

---

# Source

Based on:

The House Within™

# The Resident State

A person arriving at the Entrance Room™ may feel:

- disconnected from themselves
- uncertain about their direction
- overwhelmed by expectations
- stuck in repeated patterns
- searching for meaning
- feeling that something is missing

They may not know exactly what needs to change.

They only know they want deeper understanding.

---

# The Architect State

The person begins moving toward:

- curiosity
- awareness
- self-observation
- acceptance
- conscious choice

They begin to see:

"I am not only experiencing my life. I can understand the person experiencing it."
