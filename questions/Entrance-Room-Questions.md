# Purpose

These questions are designed to help a person begin the journey of self-exploration.

The purpose is not to provide answers.

The purpose is to create awareness, curiosity, and deeper understanding.

# Question Categories

## 1. Entry Questions

Purpose:

Help a person understand why they are beginning this journey.

Questions:

1. What brought you here at this moment in your life?

2. What is something inside you that you have been wanting to understand?

3. If your inner world could speak, what would it want you to notice?

---

## 2. Self-Awareness Questions

Purpose:

Help a person observe their current relationship with themselves.

Questions:

1. When was the last time you stopped and honestly asked yourself how you are doing?

2. What parts of your life feel intentional?

3. What parts of your life feel like they are happening automatically?

---

## 3. Autopilot Living™ Questions

Purpose:

Help identify unconscious patterns and repeated behaviours.

Questions:

1. What routines or decisions in your life have you never questioned?

2. Are you choosing your current path, or continuing a path chosen earlier?

3. Where do you feel disconnected between what you do and what you truly want?

---

## 4. Identity Questions

Purpose:

Explore identity beyond external roles and expectations.

Questions:

1. Who are you beyond your roles and responsibilities?

2. What remains when achievements and expectations are removed?

3. Which parts of yourself have you hidden to meet expectations?

---

# Reflection Depth

## Level 1 — Observation

What am I noticing?

Example:

"What parts of my life feel automatic?"

---

## Level 2 — Understanding

What might be influencing this?

Example:

"What patterns have shaped the way I respond?"

---

## Level 3 — Awareness

What does this reveal about me?

Example:

"What am I learning about myself?"

---

## Level 4 — Choice

What possibility exists now?

Example:

"What conscious choice could I make from this awareness?"

---

# Follow-Up Questions

The Mirror™ may explore:

- "When did you first notice this pattern?"
- "How does this affect the way you experience your life?"
- "What would you like to understand more deeply?"

---

# Mirror Guidance

The Mirror™ should:

- ask one question at a time
- allow silence and reflection
- encourage curiosity
- respect personal timing
- avoid judgement

---

The Mirror™ should not:

- diagnose
- force conclusions
- define the person's identity
- rush into solutions

---

# Memory Anchor

"A meaningful question does not force an answer. It opens a door."
