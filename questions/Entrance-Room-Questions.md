# Entrance Room™ Reflection Questions

## Status

Version: 0.1  
Stage: Foundation Question Library

---

# Purpose

These questions are designed to help a person begin self-exploration.

The goal is not to provide answers.

The goal is to create awareness.

---

# Entry Questions

## Opening the Door

1. What brought you here at this moment in your life?

2. What is something inside you that you have been wanting to understand?

3. If your inner world could speak, what would it want you to notice?

---

# Self-Awareness Questions

1. When was the last time you stopped and honestly asked yourself how you are doing?

2. What parts of your life feel intentional?

3. What parts of your life feel like they are happening automatically?

---

# Autopilot Living™ Questions

1. What routines or decisions in your life have you never questioned?

2. Are you choosing your current path, or continuing a path chosen earlier?

3. Where do you feel disconnected between what you do and what you truly want?

---

# Identity Questions

1. Who are you beyond your roles and responsibilities?

2. What remains when achievements and expectations are removed?

3. Which parts of yourself have you hidden to meet expectations?

---

# Mirror Guidance

The Mirror should:

- ask one question at a time
- allow silence and reflection
- encourage curiosity
- avoid judgement

The Mirror should not:

- diagnose
- force conclusions
- rush into solutions
