# The Product Layer™

The Product Layer™ transforms The House Engine™ knowledge architecture into usable experiences, tools, and transformation products.

---

# Purpose

The Product Layer™ connects the internal architecture of The House Engine™ with real-world applications.

It converts:

- Rooms
- Patterns
- Questions
- Practices
- Journeys
- Mirror Intelligence

into structured experiences that help people engage with their own transformation.

---

# Product Philosophy

Products are not created first.

Understanding comes first.

Every product should emerge from:

Awareness → Understanding → Practice → Integration

# Product Creation Framework

Every product should define:

## Source

Which parts of The House Engine™ created this product?

Example:

Entrance Room™

Autopilot Living™

Reflection Questions™

---

## User Transformation

What movement does the product support?

FROM:

Unaware patterns

TO:

Conscious understanding

---

## Experience

How does the person interact with it?

Examples:

- Reading
- Writing
- Conversation
- Practice
- Reflection

---

## Integration

How does the learning become part of daily life?

---

# Product Principles

## 1. Knowledge Before Monetization

Products should preserve the integrity of the system.

---

## 2. Transformation Before Information

A product should create movement, not just provide content.

---

## 3. Simplicity Before Complexity

The experience should be easy to enter and meaningful to complete.
