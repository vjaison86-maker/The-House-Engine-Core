# The Research Layer™

The Research Layer™ is the knowledge foundation supporting The House Engine™.

It collects, organizes, and preserves insights that help explain human patterns, identity, transformation, behavior, and personal growth.

---

# Purpose

The Research Layer™ ensures that The House Engine™ is built upon:

- observation
- learning
- evidence
- exploration
- accumulated wisdom

It connects external knowledge with the internal architecture of The House Engine™.

# Research Standards

## 1. Curiosity Before Conclusions

Research should encourage exploration rather than fixed assumptions.

---

## 2. Understanding Before Application

Knowledge should first improve understanding before becoming a tool.

---

## 3. Human-Centered Approach

Research should always consider the human experience behind the information.
