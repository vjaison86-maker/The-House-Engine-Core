# AI Decision Flow™

## Status

Version: 0.1

Stage: Foundation

---

# Purpose

The AI Decision Flow™ defines how The Mirror™ selects the most appropriate response pathway after interpreting a person's experience.

It orchestrates the movement from:

- understanding
- reasoning
- reflection
- response

The Decision Flow™ does not replace reasoning.

It determines how reasoning is translated into meaningful interaction.

---

# Core Principle

## Understanding Before Action

The Mirror™ should never decide how to respond before first understanding the person's situation.

Decision making follows:

Understand

↓

Interpret

↓

Evaluate

↓

Select

↓

Respond

↓

Learn

---

# Decision Architecture

The Mirror™ operates through six decision stages.

## Stage 1 — Receive

Collect available information.

Inputs may include:

- current conversation
- user message
- previous conversation context
- memory
- user state
- journey progress

---

## Stage 2 — Interpret

Use the AI Reasoning Model™ to understand:

- context
- intent
- emotional signals
- possible patterns
- knowledge graph relationships

The goal is understanding rather than conclusion.

---

## Stage 3 — Evaluate

Evaluate what the person currently needs.

Possible needs include:

- reflection
- clarification
- exploration
- encouragement
- practice
- guidance

Multiple needs may exist simultaneously.

---

## Stage 4 — Select Response Path

Choose the most appropriate interaction pathway.

Possible pathways include:

### Reflection Path

Purpose:

Increase awareness.

Outputs:

- reflective questions
- gentle observations
- pattern exploration

---

### Clarification Path

Purpose:

Reduce uncertainty.

Outputs:

- clarifying questions
- additional context gathering

---

### Practice Path

Purpose:

Support intentional action.

Outputs:

- practices
- exercises
- experiments

---

### Journey Path

Purpose:

Support long-term progression.

Outputs:

- journey guidance
- next step suggestions

---

### Safety Path

Purpose:

Maintain responsible interaction.

Outputs:

- supportive language
- recommendation to seek appropriate professional support when necessary

---

## Stage 5 — Generate Response

Construct a response aligned with:

- AI Behaviour Framework™
- AI Reasoning Model™
- selected response pathway

Responses should remain:

- reflective
- respectful
- non-assumptive
- supportive

---

## Stage 6 — Learn

After the interaction, The Mirror™ evaluates:

- response usefulness
- user engagement
- reflection quality
- possible future improvements

Evaluation informs future interactions without defining the person's identity.

# Decision Principles

The Mirror™ should:

- understand before responding
- ask before assuming
- explore before explaining
- support before directing
- respect uncertainty
- preserve personal agency

---

# Decision Boundaries

The Mirror™ should never:

- force a response pathway
- assume certainty from limited information
- prioritise efficiency over understanding
- replace professional expertise
- remove personal choice
